# Shareable Links

## Overview

Your portfolio has a unique, shareable URL you can send to recruiters, hiring managers, and include on your resume or LinkedIn.

## Your Portfolio URL

Format: `https://career-portfolio-manager.com/portfolio/{username}`

Example: `https://career-portfolio-manager.com/portfolio/john-smith`

## Finding Your Link

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Look for "Your Portfolio" section
3. Click **"Copy Link"** button
4. Link copied to clipboard!

## What Visitors See

Your public portfolio displays:
- ✅ Basic information (title, tagline, bio)
- ✅ Projects with descriptions
- ✅ Skills with proficiency levels
- ✅ Work history
- ✅ Certifications
- ✅ Achievements
- ✅ Contact information (email only to logged-in recruiters)

**Private information** (career preferences, applications, match scores) is **never visible** to visitors.

## Using Your Link

### On Your Resume
Add under contact information:
```
Portfolio: career-portfolio-manager.com/portfolio/john-smith
```

### On LinkedIn
- Add to "Contact Info" → "Website"
- Include in About section
- Share in posts when job hunting

### In Job Applications
Paste link in:
- Cover letters
- Application forms
- Email signatures

### When Networking
- Share in LinkedIn messages
- Include in email introductions
- Add to business cards

## Customizing Your Username

Want a cleaner URL?

1. Go to **Account Settings**
2. Update your username
3. Your portfolio URL updates automatically

**Best practices:**
- Use first-last name format
- Keep it professional
- Make it memorable
- No special characters

## Privacy Settings

### Public Visibility
By default, anyone with the link can view your portfolio.

### Recruiter-Only Mode (Optional)
Limit visibility to verified recruiters only:
1. Go to **Privacy Settings**
2. Enable "Recruiter-Only Mode"
3. Only logged-in recruiters can view

**Note**: This reduces discoverability but increases privacy.

## Tracking Portfolio Views

See who viewed your portfolio:
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Portfolio Analytics**
2. View:
   - Total views
   - Unique visitors
   - Recruiter views
   - View trends over time

Use this data to:
- Gauge interest
- Know when to follow up
- Optimize your portfolio

## Best Practices

### Keep It Updated
Review and update monthly:
- Add new projects
- Update skills
- Refresh work history
- Remove outdated information

### Test Your Link
Before sharing:
- Open in incognito browser
- Check all sections display correctly
- Verify links work
- Test on mobile

### Make It Discoverable
Add your link to:
- Email signature
- LinkedIn profile
- GitHub profile
- Twitter/X bio
- Resume header
- Business cards

### Share Strategically
- Include in every job application
- Share when networking
- Add to conference badges
- Include in speaker bios

## QR Code (Optional)

Generate a QR code for your portfolio:
1. Go to **Dashboard** → **Share**
2. Click **"Generate QR Code"**
3. Download and add to:
   - Business cards
   - Resumes
   - Presentations
   - Conference materials

## Analytics Insights

Use portfolio views to:

### Measure Interest
High views after applications = strong interest

### Time Follow-ups
View spike = good time to follow up

### Identify Hot Leads
Multiple views from same company = serious consideration

### Optimize Content
Low views = need to improve discoverability or content
