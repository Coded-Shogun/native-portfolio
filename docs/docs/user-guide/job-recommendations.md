# Job Recommendations

## Overview

The Job Recommendations feature uses AI to match you with opportunities based on your portfolio, skills, and career preferences. This is the core of our unemployment-solving mission!

## How AI Job Matching Works

Our intelligent algorithm analyzes:

1. **Your Skills** - All skills in your portfolio with proficiency levels
2. **Your Experience** - Work history, projects, and achievements
3. **Your Preferences** - Career preferences you've set
4. **Job Requirements** - What employers are looking for
5. **Market Data** - Industry standards and trends

Then it calculates a **Match Score (0-100%)** for each job.

## Accessing Job Recommendations

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **"Job Matches"** in the Job-Getting Features section
3. Or navigate to `/job-recommendations`

## Understanding Match Scores

### Score Ranges

- **90-100%: Excellent Match** 🟢
  - You meet nearly all requirements
  - High likelihood of success
  - Apply immediately!

- **75-89%: Good Match** 🟡
  - You meet most requirements
  - Some skill gaps but manageable
  - Strong candidate with preparation

- **60-74%: Fair Match** 🟠
  - You meet basic requirements
  - Several skill gaps to address
  - Consider if you're willing to learn quickly

- **Below 60%: Not Recommended** 🔴
  - Significant gaps in requirements
  - Would require major upskilling
  - Focus on better matches first

### What Affects Your Score

The match score is weighted across:

- **Skills Match (40%)** - How many required skills you have
- **Experience Level (20%)** - Years of experience alignment
- **Location (15%)** - Geographic or remote work fit
- **Work Type (10%)** - Remote/hybrid/on-site preference
- **Salary (10%)** - Compensation alignment
- **Certifications (5%)** - Relevant credentials

## Understanding Missing Skills

When a job shows "Missing Skills":

### Critical Skills
Skills marked as "required" that you don't have. These significantly lower your match score.

**Action**: Learn these before applying if possible.

### Preferred Skills
Skills marked as "nice to have" that you're lacking. Minor impact on match score.

**Action**: Mention willingness to learn in your application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How often are recommendations updated?**
A: New jobs are added throughout the day. Check daily for best results.

**Q: Why am I not seeing many matches?**
A: This could mean:
- Your career preferences are too narrow
- Your profile is incomplete
- You need to add more skills
- There are currently fewer jobs in your target roles

**Q: Can I apply to jobs with low match scores?**
A: Yes, but focus on higher matches for better success rates. Low scores indicate significant gaps.

**Q: What if I don't have a required skill but can learn quickly?**
A: Apply anyway! Mention your ability to learn quickly in your application and cite examples of fast learning from your past.
