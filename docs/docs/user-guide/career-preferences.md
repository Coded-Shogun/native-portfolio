# Career Preferences

## Overview

Career preferences tell our AI matching algorithm what kind of jobs you're looking for. Setting accurate preferences ensures you receive highly relevant job recommendations.

## Accessing Career Preferences

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **"Preferences"** in the Job-Getting Features section
3. Or navigate directly to `/career-preferences`

## Setting Your Preferences

### Job Search Status

**Are you actively seeking opportunities?**

- ✅ Check this box if you're actively job hunting
- ⬜ Uncheck if you're just exploring or not currently looking

**Note**: This doesn't make your profile public, but it helps prioritize your job matches.

### Desired Roles

**What positions are you interested in?**

Enter job titles you're targeting (comma-separated):

Examples:
- Full Stack Developer, Software Engineer
- Senior React Developer, Frontend Lead
- Data Scientist, ML Engineer
- Product Manager, Technical Product Manager

**Tips:**
- Be specific but not too narrow
- Include variations (e.g., "Software Engineer" and "Software Developer")
- List 2-5 role titles for best results

### Location Preferences

**Where do you want to work?**

Specify your preferred locations (comma-separated):

Examples:
- Remote, Hybrid
- San Francisco, New York, Remote
- Austin, Remote
- London, UK Remote

**Tips:**
- Include "Remote" if you're open to remote work
- Be specific with cities for location-based roles
- You can mix cities and work types

### Work Type Preference

Select your preferred work arrangement:

- **Remote** - Work from anywhere
- **Hybrid** - Mix of remote and office
- **On-site** - Office-based work
- **No Preference** - Open to any arrangement

### Salary Expectations

**What's your target salary range?**

- **Minimum Salary** - Your floor (e.g., $80,000)
- **Maximum Salary** - Your target (e.g., $150,000)

**Tips:**
- Research market rates for your role and location
- Be realistic but don't undervalue yourself
- This filters out jobs outside your range

### Benefits and Perks

**What benefits are important to you?** (Optional)

Common benefits to consider:
- Health insurance
- 401(k) matching
- Professional development budget
- Flexible hours
- Equity/stock options
- Unlimited PTO

### Industry Preferences

**Which industries interest you?** (Optional)

Examples:
- Technology
- Finance
- Healthcare
- E-commerce
- Education
- Gaming

## How Preferences Affect Job Matching

Your preferences influence the AI matching algorithm:

### Match Score Calculation

The algorithm considers:
- **Role match** (40%) - How well the job title matches your desired roles
- **Location match** (15%) - Geographic and work type alignment
- **Salary match** (10%) - How the salary range fits your expectations
- **Skills match** (40%) - Your skills vs. required skills (from your profile)
- **Experience match** (20%) - Your background vs. job requirements

### Filtering

Jobs that don't meet your basic criteria are filtered out:
- Minimum salary below your floor
- Location outside your preferences (unless remote)
- Work type doesn't match your preference

## Updating Your Preferences

You can update preferences anytime:

1. Go to Career Preferences page
2. Modify any fields
3. Click **"Save Preferences"**
4. Your job recommendations will update immediately

**Tip**: Review and update preferences monthly or when your job search focus changes.

## Best Practices

### Be Honest

- Set realistic salary expectations
- Only mark "actively seeking" if you truly are
- Don't add role titles you're not interested in

### Be Flexible

- Don't be too restrictive with location
- Consider hybrid if you prefer remote
- Allow some salary range flexibility

### Be Specific

- Use actual job titles from job postings
- Name specific cities, not just regions
- Specify required vs. nice-to-have benefits
