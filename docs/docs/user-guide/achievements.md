# Achievements

## Overview

Achievements highlight notable accomplishments that set you apart from other candidates.

## Adding Achievements

1. Go to **Portfolio Editor** → **Achievements** tab
2. Click **"Add Achievement"**
3. Fill in:
   - **Title** (e.g., "First Place - National Hackathon 2023")
   - **Description** (what you accomplished)
   - **Date**
   - **URL** (proof/link if available)
4. Click **"Save"**

## Types of Achievements

### Awards & Recognition
- Hackathon wins
- Employee of the Month/Year
- Industry awards
- Academic honors

### Publications & Speaking
- Blog posts or articles
- Conference talks
- Podcast appearances
- Technical documentation

### Open Source
- Major contributions to popular projects
- Maintainer of open source library
- GitHub stars on your projects

### Impact Metrics
- Products you shipped
- Users impacted
- Revenue generated
- Cost savings

## Examples

**Title**: Winner - TechCrunch Disrupt Hackathon 2023
**Description**: Led team of 4 to build AI-powered job matching platform, beating 200+ teams. Won $10,000 prize.

**Title**: Open Source Contribution to React
**Description**: Contributed performance optimization reducing re-renders by 25%. PR #12345 merged into React core.

**Title**: Speaker at ReactConf 2023
**Description**: Presented "State Management Best Practices" to audience of 500+ developers. Talk has 10K+ views on YouTube.

## Best Practices

- **Be specific** with dates and details
- **Quantify impact** when possible
- **Include links** to proof
- **Keep updated** with recent achievements
- **Relevance matters** - focus on technical/professional achievements

## Impact on Profile

Achievements demonstrate excellence and ambition, significantly boosting recruiter interest.
