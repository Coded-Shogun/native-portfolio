# Application Tracking

## Overview

The Application Tracking feature helps you manage your entire job search pipeline in one place. Track every application from submission to offer, stay organized, and optimize your job search strategy.

## Accessing Application Tracking

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **"My Applications"** in the Job-Getting Features section
3. Or navigate to `/my-applications`

## Application Statuses

Your applications move through these stages:

### 📤 Submitted
**Definition**: Application sent, waiting for initial review

**Typical Duration**: 1-7 days

**Your Actions:**
- Follow up if no response after 1 week
- Continue applying to other positions
- Prepare for potential screening call

**Expected Next Steps:**
- Move to "Reviewing" or "Interview"
- Possible rejection

---

### 👀 Reviewing
**Definition**: Company is actively reviewing your application

**Typical Duration**: 3-14 days

**Your Actions:**
- Prepare for potential interviews
- Research the company deeply
- Review your portfolio for discussion points

**Expected Next Steps:**
- Invitation to interview
- Technical assessment
- Rejection with feedback

---

### 💼 Interview
**Definition**: Scheduled or completed interviews (phone, technical, on-site)

**Typical Duration**: 2-6 weeks

**Substages:**
- Phone screening
- Technical assessment
- Technical interview
- Behavioral interview
- Final round / On-site

**Your Actions:**
- Prepare thoroughly for each stage
- Send thank-you emails after interviews
- Follow up on timeline
- Keep interviewing elsewhere (don't wait!)

**Expected Next Steps:**
- Additional interview rounds
- Offer
- Rejection

---

### 📝 Assessment
**Definition**: Take-home assignment or coding challenge in progress

**Typical Duration**: 3-7 days for submission + 7-14 days for review

**Your Actions:**
- Allocate sufficient time for quality work
- Follow instructions exactly
- Submit before deadline
- Document your approach

**Expected Next Steps:**
- Review discussion interview
- Move to interview rounds
- Offer or rejection

---

### 🎉 Offer
**Definition**: Received job offer, negotiating or deciding

**Typical Duration**: 1-2 weeks for decision

**Your Actions:**
- Review offer details carefully
- Negotiate if appropriate
- Compare with other offers
- Ask questions before accepting
- Request offer in writing

**Expected Next Steps:**
- Accept offer → Accepted
- Decline offer → Declined
- Continue negotiations

---

### ✅ Accepted
**Definition**: Offer accepted, preparing to start

**Your Actions:**
- Complete background checks
- Submit required documents
- Plan your start date
- Give notice to current employer

**Congratulations! 🎊**

---

### ❌ Rejected
**Definition**: Application was not successful

**Your Actions:**
- Request feedback if possible
- Identify areas to improve
- Update skills based on feedback
- Don't take it personally - keep applying!

**Learn and Move Forward:**
- Review what could be improved
- Update portfolio based on feedback
- Re-run skills gap analysis if skill-related

## Best Practices

### Apply Consistently

- Set a goal (e.g., 10 applications per week)
- Track your weekly application count
- Focus on quality over quantity

### Update Status Promptly

- Update status when you receive emails
- Add notes after interviews
- Set follow-up reminders

### Analyze Patterns

Review your stats monthly:
- Which match scores get interviews?
- What's your average time in each stage?
- Where do you typically get rejected?
- Which types of companies respond?

Use insights to refine strategy!

### Follow Up Strategically

**When to Follow Up:**
- 1 week after submission (if no response)
- 2-3 days after interview (thank you + timeline check)
- At the date they gave you (if they said "we'll let you know by...")
- When status seems stalled (2+ weeks in "Reviewing")

**How to Follow Up:**
- Email HR contact or recruiter
- Keep it brief and professional
- Reiterate interest
- Ask about timeline

### Don't Wait - Keep Applying

**Never stop applying until you've accepted an offer!**

Even if you have:
- An interview scheduled
- An assessment in progress
- An "expected offer"

Continue applying - many opportunities fall through.

### Learn from Rejections

After rejection:
- Request feedback (politely)
- Identify skill gaps highlighted
- Update your approach
- Run skills gap analysis
- Keep momentum - apply to 2 more jobs immediately

## Time Management

### Application Stages & Time Allocation

**Submitted → Reviewing (1-2 weeks)**
- Spend 20% of time following up
- Spend 80% applying to new jobs

**Interview Stage (2-6 weeks)**
- Spend 60% preparing for interviews
- Spend 40% still applying elsewhere

**Offer Stage (1-2 weeks)**
- Spend 70% evaluating/negotiating offer
- Spend 30% keeping backup options warm

## Success Tips

### Momentum is Key

Apply to multiple jobs each week. Momentum creates opportunities:
- More applications = more chances
- Multiple interviews = better negotiation position
- Active pipeline reduces desperation

### Track Everything

Use notes field to record:
- Who you spoke with
- Topics discussed
- Questions they asked
- Your answers
- Their concerns
- Next steps

This helps you prepare for subsequent rounds.

### Set Weekly Goals

Example goals:
- Submit 5-10 applications
- Complete 1-2 interviews
- Follow up on 3 pending applications
- Update portfolio with 1 new project
- Learn 1 new skill from skills gap analysis

### Network in Parallel

While tracking applications:
- Connect with employees at target companies
- Ask for referrals
- Attend industry events
- Engage on LinkedIn

Referrals can move you from "Submitted" to "Interview" quickly!

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How many applications should I submit?**
A: Aim for 5-10 quality applications per week. Focus on 75%+ match scores.

**Q: When should I follow up on an application?**
A: 1 week after submission if no response. After interviews, send thank you within 24 hours and check timeline.

**Q: Should I apply to jobs with low match scores?**
A: Occasionally, but focus 80%+ of effort on 70%+ match scores for better conversion rates.

**Q: How long does the average job search take?**
A: For tech roles: 2-4 months on average. Factors: market conditions, match score, skill gaps, application volume.

**Q: What's a good response rate?**
A: 20-40% response rate is good. If lower, check match scores and profile completeness.

**Q: When should I negotiate salary?**
A: After receiving a written offer, before accepting. Research market rates first. [Read our negotiation guide](link).
