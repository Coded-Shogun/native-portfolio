# Basic Information

## Overview

Your basic information is the first thing visitors see on your portfolio. It creates the crucial first impression and helps the AI match you with relevant jobs.

## Accessing Basic Information Editor

1. Go to your **Dashboard**
2. Click **"Edit Portfolio"**
3. Navigate to the **"Basic Info"** tab

## Fields

### Professional Title

**What it is**: Your current role or desired position

**Examples:**
- Full Stack Developer
- Senior React Engineer
- Data Scientist
- Product Manager
- UX/UI Designer

**Tips:**
- Use industry-standard titles
- Be specific but not too narrow
- Match titles in job postings you're interested in
- This field heavily influences job matching!

### Tagline

**What it is**: A catchy one-liner about what you do or your unique value proposition

**Character limit**: 100-150 characters

**Examples:**
- "Building scalable web applications with React and Node.js"
- "Turning data into actionable insights | Python | ML"
- "Full-stack developer passionate about clean code and great UX"
- "Product manager shipping features users love"

**Tips:**
- Keep it concise and impactful
- Include key technologies or skills
- Show your personality
- Make it memorable

### Bio/About Me

**What it is**: 2-3 paragraphs about your background, expertise, and goals

**Word count**: 150-300 words

**What to include:**

**Paragraph 1 - Who You Are:**
- Years of experience
- Current role or situation
- Core expertise areas

**Paragraph 2 - What You Do:**
- Key technologies and skills
- Types of projects you work on
- Industries or domains
- Notable achievements

**Paragraph 3 - What You're Looking For:**
- Career goals
- Types of opportunities
- Values and work style

**Example:**

```
I'm a Full Stack Developer with 5 years of experience building scalable
web applications. Currently, I specialize in modern JavaScript
frameworks and cloud architecture, with a strong focus on performance
and user experience.

My technical toolkit includes React, Node.js, TypeScript, PostgreSQL,
and AWS. I've built everything from e-commerce platforms to real-time
collaboration tools, always with an emphasis on clean code and
maintainable architecture. I'm particularly proud of reducing load
times by 60% for a major SaaS application serving 50,000+ users.

I'm looking for senior engineering roles where I can contribute to
impactful products while mentoring junior developers. I thrive in
collaborative environments that value code quality, continuous learning,
and innovative problem-solving.
```

**Tips:**
- Write in first person ("I am" not "John is")
- Use specific numbers and achievements
- Be authentic and personable
- Include keywords relevant to your target jobs
- Show passion and enthusiasm

### Contact Information (Optional)

- **Location**: City/Region or "Remote"
- **Email**: Professional email address
- **LinkedIn**: Your LinkedIn profile URL
- **GitHub**: Your GitHub profile URL
- **Website**: Personal website or blog
- **Twitter/X**: Professional Twitter handle

**Privacy Note**: Email is only visible to logged-in recruiters viewing your public portfolio.

## Saving Changes

After editing:

1. Review all fields
2. Check for typos
3. Click **"Save Changes"**
4. Changes are live immediately on your public portfolio

## SEO and Discoverability

Your basic information affects how recruiters find you:

### Keywords

Include relevant keywords naturally:
- Technologies (React, Python, AWS)
- Methodologies (Agile, TDD, DevOps)
- Industries (FinTech, Healthcare, E-commerce)
- Roles (Frontend, Backend, Full Stack)

### Title Optimization

Your professional title is heavily weighted in search. Make it count!

**Good Examples:**
- "Senior Full Stack Developer (React, Node.js, AWS)"
- "Data Scientist | Machine Learning | Python"
- "Product Manager | B2B SaaS | Agile"

**Avoid:**
- Vague titles ("Tech Enthusiast")
- Made-up titles ("Code Ninja")
- Generic titles without context ("Developer")

## Best Practices

### Do:
- ✅ Use specific, searchable keywords
- ✅ Quantify achievements (years, percentages, numbers)
- ✅ Show personality and passion
- ✅ Update regularly as you grow
- ✅ Proofread carefully
- ✅ Match language used in job postings

### Don't:
- ❌ Use buzzwords without substance
- ❌ Write in third person
- ❌ Make unverifiable claims
- ❌ Copy-paste generic content
- ❌ Leave fields empty
- ❌ Use informal language

## Impact on Job Matching

Your basic information affects AI job matching:

- **Title**: 30% weight - Must match target roles
- **Bio keywords**: 20% weight - Technologies and skills mentioned
- **Years of experience**: 15% weight - Mentioned or implied in bio

**Optimize these for better match scores!**
