# Registration

## Creating Your Account

Getting started with Career Portfolio Manager is quick and easy!

### Step 1: Navigate to Registration

1. Visit the Career Portfolio Manager homepage
2. Click **"Get Started"** or **"Sign Up"**

### Step 2: Fill in Your Details

Provide the following information:

- **First Name** - Your first name
- **Last Name** - Your last name
- **Email Address** - A valid email address (used for login and notifications)
- **Password** - Must be at least 8 characters with uppercase, lowercase, and numbers
- **Confirm Password** - Re-enter your password to confirm

### Step 3: Submit Registration

Click **"Create Account"** to submit your registration.

## Email Verification

After registration, you'll receive a verification email:

1. Check your inbox for an email from Career Portfolio Manager
2. Click the verification link in the email
3. Your account will be activated and you can log in

**Note**: Check your spam folder if you don't see the email within a few minutes.

## First Login

Once your email is verified:

1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

## Security Tips

- Use a strong, unique password
- Don't share your login credentials
- Keep your email address up to date
- Log out when using shared computers

## Troubleshooting

### Didn't receive verification email?

- Check your spam/junk folder
- Verify you entered the correct email address
- Contact support if the issue persists

### Password requirements not met?

Your password must:
- Be at least 8 characters long
- Contain at least one uppercase letter
- Contain at least one lowercase letter
- Contain at least one number
- Optionally include special characters (!@#$%^&*)
