# Introduction

Welcome to **Career Portfolio Manager** - your AI-powered platform to solve unemployment and accelerate your career growth!

## What is Career Portfolio Manager?

Career Portfolio Manager is more than just a portfolio website builder. It's a comprehensive job-getting engine designed to help professionals:

- **Showcase their work** - Create stunning portfolios with projects, certifications, work history, and achievements
- **Get AI-matched to jobs** - Our intelligent algorithm matches you with opportunities based on your skills and preferences
- **Identify skill gaps** - Understand what skills you need to learn for your dream role
- **Track applications** - Manage your job search pipeline in one place
- **Share professionally** - Generate shareable links to send to recruiters and hiring managers

## How It Helps You Get Hired

Unlike traditional portfolio sites or job boards, Career Portfolio Manager actively helps you land jobs through:

1. **AI-Powered Job Matching** - Get personalized job recommendations with match scores showing exactly how well you fit each role
2. **Skills Gap Analysis** - Identify missing skills and get learning recommendations for your target roles
3. **Application Tracking** - Keep track of every application from submission to offer
4. **Profile Optimization** - Get real-time feedback on your profile completion to maximize visibility

## Who Is This For?

Career Portfolio Manager is perfect for:

- Software developers and engineers
- Designers and creative professionals
- Data scientists and analysts
- Product managers
- Marketing professionals
- Any professional looking to showcase their work and find better opportunities
